

# Ivy Salon — High-End Hair Studio Website

A luxurious, mobile-first single-page website for "Ivy Salon" with an elegant, minimalist aesthetic using serif typography, muted gold accents, and generous whitespace.

---

## Design System
- **Colors:** White (#FFFFFF), Cream (#F9F9F5), Charcoal (#333333), Muted Gray (#777777), Gold accent (#D4AF37), Black buttons
- **Fonts:** Playfair Display (headings) + Lato (body) via Google Fonts
- **Style:** Luxury, feminine, minimalist with generous spacing

---

## Sections (Top to Bottom)

### 1. Sticky Header
- Hamburger menu (left), "Ivy Salon" serif logo (center), search + cart icons (right)
- Backdrop blur on scroll

### 2. Hero Section
- Full-width salon/model image with subtle gradient overlay
- Glamorous, inviting atmosphere

### 3. "Your Satisfaction Is Our Priority"
- Centered serif heading + mission paragraph
- 3-column grid of service image cards (Cut, Dye, Treatment)

### 4. "We Will Change How You Look"
- Asymmetric layout: circular image (left) + text with icon features (right)
- Online Appointment & 24/7 Support callouts
- "Discover More" outlined button

### 5. Service Menu — "We Are Proud Of Our Service"
- 4 arch-shaped image cards (rounded top, flat bottom)
- Haircutting, Coloring, Treatment, Styling with descriptions + "Read More" links

### 6. "What We Provide" (Amenities)
- Split layout: gold icon amenity list (Wi-Fi, Parking, AC, Coffee) on left
- Semi-circle styled image on right

### 7. Team Section
- 3 circular portraits with names and roles
- Susan Smith, Mary Doe, Sharon Yu

### 8. Testimonial Block
- Quote with large quotation mark, client name + avatar (left)
- Arch-shaped stylist photo (right)

### 9. Pricing Table
- Clean list: service name with dotted line leading to price
- Hair Cut $50, Styling $100, etc.

### 10. Contact & Appointment
- Parallax/blurred salon background
- Floating white card with hours + address
- "MAKE APPOINTMENT" CTA button

### 11. Latest News (Blog)
- Two vertical cards with image, date badge, title, "Read More"

### 12. Newsletter & Footer
- Magnolia flower background image
- Email subscription with underline input
- Mobile-style bottom nav bar (Home, Discover, Cart, Profile)

---

## Mobile Menu
- Slide-in drawer with navigation links to each section

## Responsive Behavior
- Mobile-first, stacking layouts on small screens
- Grid/flex adjustments for tablet and desktop
